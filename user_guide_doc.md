# User Guide - Insurance Verification Submission

## Overview
This guide explains how to submit your insurance information for verification.

**What You'll Need:**
- Your insurance card (front and back)
- A smartphone or camera to take photos
- 5-10 minutes of your time

---

## Step-by-Step Instructions

### Step 1: Gather Your Information

Before starting, have ready:
- ✅ Your full legal name
- ✅ Your date of birth
- ✅ Your insurance card (physical card)
- ✅ Your email address
- ✅ Your phone number

---

### Step 2: Take Clear Photos of Your Insurance Card

**Important:** Clear photos help us verify your insurance faster!

#### Photo Tips for Best Results

**Good Photo Examples:**
- ✅ Card fills most of the image
- ✅ All text is readable
- ✅ No shadows or glare
- ✅ Card is flat (not bent)
- ✅ Good lighting (natural light is best)
- ✅ Card is in focus

**What to Avoid:**
- ❌ Blurry images
- ❌ Photos too dark or too bright
- ❌ Glare from flash or lights
- ❌ Partial card (edges cut off)
- ❌ Card at an angle
- ❌ Fingers covering text

#### How to Take a Good Photo

1. **Find Good Lighting**
   - Use natural light if possible
   - Avoid direct sunlight or harsh shadows
   - Turn off flash unless absolutely needed

2. **Position Your Card**
   - Lay card flat on a dark surface
   - Make sure all edges are visible
   - Keep card straight (not tilted)

3. **Take the Photo**
   - Hold phone steady (about 6-12 inches above card)
   - Make sure all text is in focus
   - Take photo when stable

4. **Check Your Photo**
   - Zoom in to verify all text is readable
   - Retake if blurry or unclear
   - Take photos of both front AND back

---

### Step 3: Fill Out the Form

**1. Go to the verification form**
   - Visit: [your-website-url.com]
   - Click "Verify Insurance"

**2. Enter Your Personal Information**

**Full Name:**
- Enter exactly as shown on insurance card
- Include middle name if on card
- Example: "John Robert Smith" not "John Smith"

**Date of Birth:**
- Format: MM/DD/YYYY
- Example: 03/15/1985
- Must match what's on file with insurance

**Member ID:**
- Find on your insurance card (usually front)
- Enter exactly as shown
- Include letters, numbers, dashes
- Example: "ABC-123-456" not "ABC123456"
- Common locations:
  - Front of card, labeled "Member ID"
  - May also be called "Subscriber ID"
  - Different from Group Number

**Insurance Company Name:**
- Enter full official name
- Examples:
  - "Blue Cross Blue Shield of Texas"
  - "UnitedHealthcare"
  - "Aetna"
- Check your card for exact name

**Email Address:**
- We'll send updates here
- Example: john.smith@email.com
- Double-check for typos

**Phone Number:**
- Include area code
- Example: (555) 123-4567
- We'll only call if needed

---

### Step 4: Upload Insurance Card Photos

**Front of Card:**
1. Click "Upload Front" button
2. Select your front photo
3. Wait for upload to complete
4. You'll see a preview - verify it's clear

**Back of Card:**
1. Click "Upload Back" button
2. Select your back photo
3. Wait for upload to complete
4. Preview should show readable text

**Supported Formats:**
- JPG/JPEG
- PNG
- Maximum size: 5MB per photo

**If Upload Fails:**
- Photo might be too large (compress it)
- Check internet connection
- Try different browser
- Make sure photo is JPG or PNG

**Before Clicking Submit:**

- ✅ Verify Member ID matches card exactly
- ✅ Confirm date of birth is correct
- ✅ Check both photos are clear and readable
- ✅ Review email address for typos

**Click "Submit" Button**

### Step 6: Confirmation

**You'll See:**

## What If You Get an Error?

### "Please Upload Clearer Images"

**Why this happens:**
- Photos too blurry
- Text not readable
- Card edges cut off
- Poor lighting

**What to do:**
1. Take new photos following our tips above
2. Make sure text is sharp and clear
3. Use better lighting
4. Hold camera steady
5. Resubmit with new photos

---

### "Member ID Doesn't Match"

**Why this happens:**
- Typo in Member ID field
- Used wrong ID number (Group Number vs Member ID)
- Spaces or dashes in wrong places

**What to do:**
1. Check your insurance card carefully
2. Find the Member ID (not Group Number)
3. Enter exactly as shown
4. Include all dashes, spaces, letters
5. Resubmit with correct ID

**Common Mistakes:**
- ❌ Using Group Number instead of Member ID
- ❌ Missing dashes: "ABC123456" should be "ABC-123-456"
- ❌ Extra spaces: "ABC 123" should be "ABC123"

---

### "Insurance Not Active"

**Why this happens:**
- Coverage has ended
- Policy not yet effective
- Member no longer eligible

**What to do:**
1. Check your insurance card effective dates
2. Contact your insurance company to verify status
3. Our team will call you to discuss:
   - Cash payment options
   - Payment plans
   - Alternative insurance

## Tips for Success

### Before You Submit

1. **Have Good Lighting**
   - Natural light works best
   - Avoid shadows and glare

2. **Double-Check Everything**
   - Member ID exactly matches card
   - Date of birth is correct
   - Email has no typos

3. **Take Multiple Photos**
   - Try 2-3 photos of each side
   - Pick the clearest one
   - Delete blurry ones

### Common Member ID Locations

**Front of Card:**
- Usually labeled "Member ID" or "ID Number"
- Typically 8-15 characters
- May include letters and numbers
- Example: ABC123456789

**Not the Same As:**
- Group Number (usually shorter)
- Rx Bin Number (for prescriptions)
- Plan Number

**Still Can't Find It?**
- Look for "Subscriber ID"
- Check back of card
- Call number on card for help
